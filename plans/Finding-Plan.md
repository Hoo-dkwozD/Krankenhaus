## Exhaustive completion rule

An objective is complete only after every mapped endpoint, method, protocol, input, relevant parser/context variant, reachable role, and distinct valid instance has been tested or explicitly marked blocked. `Negative for tested boundary` applies only to the recorded boundary, not to the application as a whole.
