## Review rules

1. Resolve the current online source rather than relying on memory or a stale summary.
2. Record the title, source, version/date, relevant sections, and the exact technique or control extracted.
3. Check applicability against the target technology, parser, browser, framework, protocol, input context, and role.
4. Record limitations, licensing, stale content, unavailable pages, and any technique excluded from the current test.
5. Map each selected technique to an approved finding, endpoint/input, role, and test batch.
6. Do not send an entire payload or technique collection automatically.
